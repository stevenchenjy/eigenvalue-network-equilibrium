# Academic integrity and software provenance

This computational workspace was created with AI assistance for exploratory mathematical learning and prototyping. Approval to use its methods, calculations or figures in assessed work has not been recorded.

- Code, tests, configurations and generated outputs are exploratory aids. Numerical correctness and public availability do not grant academic-use permission.
- Assessed mathematical explanations, proofs, findings and conclusions must be independently authored by the student. Do not automatically insert generated results or figures into a submission-ready manuscript.
- Keep study support and exploratory computation separate from assessed writing. Preserve independently authored text without automatic rewriting.
- Record AI assistance, actual inputs, source/version identity, metric/threshold, outputs and independent checks. Every run defaults to exploratory, unapproved status.
- A reference check is software validation rather than a new mathematical discovery. Do not invent source reading, results, research progress or instructor decisions.
- Obtain and record the instructor's specific decision before proposed assessed use. Approval for a method and permission to include a particular output can have different scopes.

Use [the instructor checklist](docs/instructor_approval_checklist.md) to prepare that discussion. Initial origin and input hashes are in [software_origin.json](docs/software_origin.json). Publication on GitHub is a software-delivery action; it does not change the approval status or the origin of any material.
